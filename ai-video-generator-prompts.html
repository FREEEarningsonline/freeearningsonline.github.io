The Golden Formula for AI Video Prompts

To prevent the AI from generating chaotic, morphing videos, you must dictate every single variable of the scene. The AI needs to know how the camera behaves, what the subject is doing, and what the environment feels like. Use this 5-step framework:

  • Camera Movement: Start your prompt with how the camera moves (e.g., Drone shot, slow pan left, steadycam tracking shot, zoom in).
  • Subject & Action: Who or what is in the frame, and exactly what are they doing? (e.g., A cyberpunk samurai unsheathing a glowing katana). Keep the action simple and linear.
  • Environment/Setting: Where is this happening? (e.g., In a rainy, neon-lit alleyway in Tokyo).
  • Lighting & Atmosphere: (e.g., Volumetric fog, moody cinematic lighting, high contrast, golden hour).
  • Technical Quality: End with video quality tags (e.g., Photorealistic, 8k resolution, 35mm lens, depth of field, motion blur).

Top 5 Cinematic Video Prompts to Copy & Paste

Here are five highly tested prompts you can use right now on Runway Gen-3 or Kling AI to get mind-blowing results. Feel free to tweak the bracketed words to fit your project.

1. The Epic Drone Reveal (Great for Intro/Outro)

FPV drone flying rapidly through a dense, misty pine forest at sunrise, emerging suddenly over a massive, breathtaking waterfall. Golden hour lighting, volumetric rays piercing through the trees, ultra-realistic, cinematic motion, 4k resolution, shot on RED Digital Cinema.

2. The Emotional Close-Up (Great for Storytelling)

Extreme close-up shot of an elderly man with deep wrinkles and kind eyes, looking directly into the camera and slightly smiling. The background is a highly blurred bustling city street at night with bokeh neon lights. Slow push-in camera movement, 85mm lens, f/1.4 aperture, photorealistic skin texture, dramatic cinematic lighting.

3. The Fast-Paced Car Chase (High Action)

Low-angle tracking shot attached to the bumper of a sleek matte-black sports car drifting around a tight corner on a wet mountain road. Sparks flying from the tires, heavy rain, dark stormy sky, intense motion blur, hyper-realistic, 60fps smooth motion, adrenaline-pumping scene.

4. The Sci-Fi Concept (For TikTok/Reels)

Steadycam walking shot behind a futuristic astronaut exploring the glowing, bioluminescent ruins of an alien civilization. Huge glowing mushrooms, floating particles in the air, purple and teal cinematic color grading, Unreal Engine 5 render style, hyper-detailed, slow and mysterious movement.

5. Product Commercial / Macro Shot

Macro photography shot of a sleek glass perfume bottle resting on a dark marble surface. A single drop of water slowly rolls down the side of the glass. Soft studio lighting, dark moody background, highly reflective, commercial product advertisement style, 8k, photorealistic.

Runway Gen-3 vs Kling AI: Which Should You Use?

If you are serious about making money with AI videos, you need to know which tool fits your needs.

Runway Gen-3 Alpha: Best for absolute photorealism, commercial ads, and Hollywood-style cinematic shots. It adheres incredibly well to exact camera movement prompts. If you are doing freelance commercial work, Runway is the gold standard.

Kling AI: Known for handling highly complex, long-duration physics. If you want a video of a person eating a bowl of noodles or a dog playing with a ball (actions that require complex object interaction), Kling AI currently handles these multi-step actions with fewer morphing glitches than its competitors.
