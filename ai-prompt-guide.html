AI Prompt Usage Guide

1. Structure of a Perfect Image Prompt

To get photorealistic results, structure your prompts as: [Subject] + [Environment/Background] + [Lighting Condition] + [Camera/Style Shot] + [Aspect Ratio Parameter].

2. Aspect Ratio Settings

  • --ar 16:9 - Ideal for YouTube Thumbnails and Desktop Banners.
  • --ar 9:16 - Optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Shorts.
  • --ar 1:1 - Recommended for Profile Avatars and Square posts.

3. Negative Prompts

Incorporate negative prompts such as blurry, distorted fingers, low resolution, overexposed to exclude common AI artifacts.
