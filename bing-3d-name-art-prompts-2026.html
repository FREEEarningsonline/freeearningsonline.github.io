Why 3D Name Art is Dominating Social Media in 2026

Personalized 3D digital avatars have become the most popular profile picture choice across Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp. Using Microsoft’s free Bing Image Creator (Designer), creators can produce realistic 3D characters sitting on social media logos with illuminated neon name displays.

The secret to getting crisp 3D text without typos lies in prompt formatting: using capital letters inside double quotes ("YOUR NAME") and specifying clothing, environment, and lighting setup explicitly.

How to Use These Prompts on Bing (Step-by-Step)

  1. Visit the official Microsoft Bing Image Creator website (bing.com/create) or open the Copilot App.
  2. Log in with your free Microsoft account.
  3. Copy any prompt from this page and replace [YOUR NAME] with your actual name or handle.
  4. Click "Create" and wait 10-15 seconds for AI to generate 4 variations.
  5. Select your favorite image and hit Download in high resolution.
