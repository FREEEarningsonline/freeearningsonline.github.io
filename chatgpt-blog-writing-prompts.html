ChatGPT Prompts for Blog Writing: A Simple Framework

By Nazim Mustafa · Updated October 10, 2026

ChatGPT can speed up blog writing, but a vague request such as "write a blog about fitness" returns vague text. The fix is to give the model the same information a human freelancer would need. This article shows a four-part framework and how to keep the final post genuinely yours.

The RTCF framework

  • Role: "You are an experienced travel blogger."
  • Task: "Write an outline for a post about budget trips in Pakistan."
  • Context: audience, tone, word count, what readers already know.
  • Format: headings, bullet points, table, or plain paragraphs.

Example prompt

You are an experienced travel blogger. Create an outline for a 1,000-word post titled "5 Budget Trips Under Rs. 15,000". Audience: college students. Tone: friendly and practical. Format: H2 headings with two bullet points each.

Work in stages, not one shot

  1. Ask for 10 title ideas and choose one.
  2. Ask for an outline and edit it yourself.
  3. Ask for one section at a time, giving your own notes and facts.
  4. Ask ChatGPT to critique your draft for gaps, not to rewrite it.

Keeping content original

Search engines and AdSense reviewers look for added value. Raw AI output is the same text thousands of people receive. Add personal experience, real numbers you collected, your own photos and opinions. Always fact-check names, prices and dates, because the model can state wrong details confidently.

Useful follow-up prompts

  • "Rewrite this paragraph in simpler English for beginners."
  • "List five questions a reader might still have after this section."
  • "Suggest a meta description under 155 characters."
